# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated**: December 18, 2025

## 1. Introduction

CompileStrength ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy
explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you
use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Information**: Email address, password (encrypted), name
- **Profile Data**: Age, weight, height, fitness goals, experience level
- **Workout Data**: Exercise logs, sets, reps, weights, workout programs
- **Preferences**: Unit preferences (lbs/kg), rest timer settings
- **Billing Information**: Processed through LemonSqueezy (we do not store
  payment details)

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, features used, time spent
- **Device Information**: Browser type, operating system, IP address
- **Location Data**: General location via Cloudflare geolocation
  (country/region)
- **Session Data**: Login times, authentication events

### 2.3 Cookies and Tracking

- Session cookies for authentication
- Analytics cookies to understand usage patterns
- Preference cookies to remember your settings

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and improve the Service
- Generate personalized AI workout recommendations
- Track your fitness progress
- Process subscription payments
- Send service-related communications
- Respond to support requests
- Ensure security and prevent fraud
- Comply with legal obligations

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal information. We share data only with:

### 4.1 Service Providers

- **LemonSqueezy**: Payment processing and subscription management
- **Neon Database**: Secure data storage (PostgreSQL)
- **Cloudflare**: Hosting and content delivery
- **OpenAI**: AI workout generation (anonymized data only)

### 4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law or to:

- Comply with legal process
- Protect our rights and property
- Prevent fraud or security issues
- Protect user safety

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Encryption at rest for sensitive data
- Password hashing with secure algorithms
- Regular security audits
- Access controls and authentication

However, no method is 100% secure. Use strong passwords and protect your account
credentials.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Active Accounts**: Data retained while your account is active
- **Cancelled Accounts**: Data retained for 90 days after cancellation, then
  deleted
- **Billing Records**: Retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance
- **Workout Data**: Retained until account deletion (or upon request)

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access**: Request a copy of your data
- **Correction**: Update inaccurate information
- **Deletion**: Request account and data deletion
- **Export**: Download your workout data
- **Opt-Out**: Unsubscribe from marketing emails
- **Portability**: Receive data in machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, contact: eliaz@bobadilla.tech

## 8. Children's Privacy

CompileStrength is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect
data from children. If we discover we have collected data from a child, we will
delete it immediately.

## 9. International Users

Your data may be transferred to and stored in the United States and other
countries. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

## 10. Third-Party Links

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible
for their privacy practices. Review their privacy policies before providing
information.

## 11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights:

- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to know if personal information is sold or shared
- Right to opt-out of sale (we do not sell data)
- Right to deletion
- Right to non-discrimination

## 12. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are in the EU, you have rights under GDPR:

- **Legal Basis**: Consent, contract performance, legitimate interests
- **Data Protection Officer**: eliaz@bobadilla.tech
- **Right to Lodge Complaint**: Contact your local data protection authority

## 13. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of significant changes via
email or Service notification. Continued use after changes constitutes
acceptance.
